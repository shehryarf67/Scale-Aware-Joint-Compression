3. FINAL LAYER OBJECTIVE, JOINT vs SEQUENTIAL, MATCHED PER LAYER
===============================================================================

  ** `relative_improvement` is NOT comparable across arms and is deliberately not used
  here.** Each arm measures it against its own naive baseline, and the arms do not share
  one. Q->P quantises first, so at pythia-1b/moderate its naive_loss is 9,548 against the
  joint arm's 1,635,000 -- the same layer, a reference three orders of magnitude apart.
  Dividing by it produced an apparent -6921% 'efficiency' for sequential, which is an
  artefact of the denominator and not a property of the solver.

  `final_loss` IS comparable: it is the same quantity at the end of the same layer. Below
  is the mean per-layer relative difference, (seq - joint) / seq, matched layer by layer
  and replicate by replicate. Positive => joint ends at a lower objective.

  scale         budget       bits  joint better by  layers joint wins   R
  pythia-160m   moderate        8         0.0039%        207/384       8
  pythia-160m   aggressive      4         2.1647%        367/384       8
  pythia-410m   moderate        8        -0.4911%        373/768       8
  pythia-410m   aggressive      4         2.2826%        752/768       8
  pythia-1b     moderate        8         0.1668%        277/320       5
  pythia-1b     aggressive      4         2.3214%        317/320       5

  This is the LOCAL objective. It is not the model-level result and need not agree with
  it: layers are optimised independently, and a lower sum of layer objectives does not
  guarantee lower end-to-end perplexity.
